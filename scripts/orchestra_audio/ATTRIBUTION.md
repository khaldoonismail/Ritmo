Real instrument recordings used by `scripts/create_instrument_game.mjs`. Each
clip is ~10 seconds of actual playing (a scale, run, improvisation, or solo
performance — not a single held note). Where one source wasn't long enough
on its own, two real recordings of the same instrument (often the same
performer/session) were concatenated to reach 10s; this is noted below.

## University of Iowa Musical Instrument Samples (theremin.music.uiowa.edu)

Public domain — "freely available ... and may be downloaded and used for
any projects, without restrictions."

- bassoon — Bassoon.ff.C3Bb3.aiff
  https://theremin.music.uiowa.edu/MISbassoon.html
- tuba — Tuba.ff.C2B2.aiff
  https://theremin.music.uiowa.edu/MIStuba.html
